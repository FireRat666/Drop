Drop

Stand on the right colour before the floor falls away.

A big grid of coloured tiles hangs in space. Each round the screens show a colour and a countdown. Get to a tile of that colour in time, because every other tile drops. The countdown gets shorter every round, and the last player standing wins.

Play it

Drop is a whole room, not a game you add to your own world. Open it as a space in VR or on desktop:

  • Altspace: type drop.firer.at into the search bar, or join it from its SideQuest page.
  • Banter: open https://drop.firer.at as a space.

The game runs entirely in the space, so there is no server and nothing to install.

How to play

  1. The host clicks START GAME. Everyone in the lobby is teleported onto the grid.
  2. Watch the big screens above the arena for the target colour and the countdown.
  3. Stand on any tile of that colour before the countdown ends.
  4. Every other tile drops. If you were on one, you fall back to the lobby.
  5. The tiles rise again and the next round starts, with less time.
  6. The last player standing wins. Playing alone? See how long you can last.

Controls

The control panels are in the lobby. Host controls only show for the host, and anyone can claim host.

  • Claim Host: take over the game settings. If the host is still here, it takes 30 seconds.
  • Tile size: big 8x8 tiles or small 12x12 tiles (lobby only).
  • Initial timer: the first round lasts 10, 7 or 5 seconds.
  • Hard mode: the colours reshuffle every round and the timer speeds up faster.
  • Reset: end the game and go back to the lobby.
  • Muted: turn the countdown ticks off, just for you.

Scoreboard

The scoreboard in the lobby keeps most falls, and best survival times for normal and hard mode, for each tile size. Scores stay with the room.
